About Our Academy

Building Character, Knowledge & Values Since Foundation

Welcome to Parashar Educational Academy

Parashar Educational Academy is dedicated to providing quality education that blends traditional moral values with modern educational techniques. Located in Subhashgarh, Haridwar, our institution nurtures young minds from Playgroup up to Class 8th.

We believe that every child is unique and has distinct potential. Our curriculum and extracurricular ecosystem are carefully crafted to support cognitive, emotional, and social development in a safe and motivating environment.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver modern conceptual education while keeping students rooted in cultural values, discipline, and strong moral character.

🌟 Our Vision

To empower every student to become a confident, creative, and responsible future leader equipped with academic excellence.
